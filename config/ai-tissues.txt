# What each tissue value in the metadata is, as a family a question can name.
#
# Nine of eighteen datasets record no tissue at all, and GTEX records
# "Subcutaneous" and "Visceral" rather than "Adipose". A search that filters on
# the literal value therefore throws away most of the collection and then
# reports, truthfully and uselessly, that it found nothing.
#
# A value missing from here is its own family.
#
# Format:  <value in the metadata> | <family> | <what it is>

Adipose        | adipose | adipose tissue, no depot specified
Subcutaneous   | adipose | subcutaneous white adipose tissue
Visceral       | adipose | visceral white adipose tissue
Adipose.brown  | adipose | brown adipose tissue
BAT            | adipose | brown adipose tissue
Plasma         | plasma  | blood plasma
