# Value in the metadata | family | what it is
#
# RNA sequencing, microarray and proteomics are one family on purpose. A
# question about whether a gene is expressed is answered by any of them -- the
# transcript or the protein -- and naming no platform should not pick one.
# Read as RNAseq at 80% confidence, "is this gene more expressed" threw away a
# 770-sample microarray cohort that had the gene and the variable.
#
# Lipidomics is its own family because lipid species are not genes: those
# datasets carry 110 features and no symbol a gene question could match.

RNAseq            | gene or protein expression | RNA sequencing of gene expression
Array             | gene or protein expression | microarray of gene expression
Microarray        | gene or protein expression | microarray of gene expression
Proteomics        | gene or protein expression | proteomics: the protein a gene makes
Signal.lipidomics | lipidomics                 | lipidomics: lipid species, not genes
